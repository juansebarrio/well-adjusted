## What it is

The Letter Office is the Institute's postal department. It has one desk, one clerk and a drawer of stamps bought in optimism.

You give the office an address. At some point, a letter arrives there. It is written by a person, to a person, about the business of being one. It is not a newsletter in an envelope. It does not contain a discount code.

## How it works

1. You fill in the form below. It asks for a name and somewhere letters can find you.
2. The office writes. This takes as long as it takes, which is longer than email and shorter than grief.
3. A letter arrives. You may reply, keep it, or use it as a bookmark. All three are recorded as success.

## What it costs

Nothing, for now. The Institute has no funding and has decided this is a methodology.
